Why Choose SA Welding System

Based in Bhosari, Pune, SA Super Arc Welding System has manufactured and serviced industrial welding machines for over two decades. Every machine is engineered with 100% pure copper coils to withstand tough Indian workshop power fluctuations (415V ± 15%).

  • Heavy Duty & Reliable Performance Built for non-stop factory runs without premature thermal shutdown.
  • 100% Pure Electrolytic Copper Winding Superior electrical conductivity and heavy-duty IGBT inverter modules.
  • 6-Month Direct Company Warranty Full manufacturer guarantee on electrical components, parts, and build.
  • Lifetime Service Support & On-Site Visits Rapid technician dispatch to your factory across Pune and PCMC MIDCs.
  • Low Maintenance & Energy Efficient Advanced inverter circuitry significantly reduces monthly electricity costs.
